Welcome to GAN Lab

Welcome to the homepage of the Graph, Algorithm, and Network Lab at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Jammu, India. We are a group of faculty, postdocs, doctoral, graduate, and undergraduate students in the CSE Department at IIT Jammu.

Our research group works in diverse areas, including Data Management, Graph Theory, Algorithms, Optimization, Reinforcement Learning, and Game Theory. If computer science and mathematics make you feel excited, do consider joining us!

Research Themes

Core Thrusts & Focus Domains

Our members work at the intersection of mathematical theory, computational complexity, artificial intelligence, and applied distributed systems.

Graph Theory & Theoretical Computer Science

Graph isomorphism, finding copies of subgraphs in undirected signed graphs with output-sensitive guarantees, mediation in signed graphs with neutral edges, and parameterized complexity.

Signed Graphs Neutral Edges Parameterized Complexity Graph Theory

Combinatorial Optimization & Regret Minimization

Algorithmic frameworks for billboard advertising, regret-aware slot allocations, submodular function maximization, and automated decision-making under fairness constraints.

Submodular Functions Regret Minimization Billboard Advertising Optimization

Game Theory & Multi-Agent Systems

Designing scalable, fair, and efficient algorithms for strategic decision-making in multi-agent environments, competitive game dynamics, and cooperative resource sharing.

Game Theory Multi-Agent Systems Fairness Strategic Decisions

Data Management & Learned Index Structures

Modern database systems integrated with machine learning models. Designing learned index structures to enhance efficiency, query processing speed, and scalability of multi-dimensional indexing.

Learned Indexes Data Management Query Processing ML for Databases

Reinforcement Learning & Social Network Mining

Understanding and optimizing complex networked systems at the intersection of mathematics, computation, and human behaviour using reinforcement learning and data analytics.

Reinforcement Learning Social Networks Data Analytics Network Dynamics

Cloud Workflow Scheduling & Security

Resource-aware schedulers that minimize computational costs and latency while reinforcing security in single and multi-cloud environments under budget, deadline, and confidentiality constraints (ISEA Project).

Multi-Cloud Workflow Scheduling ISEA Project Cloud Security
Leadership

Principal Investigator & Lab In-Charge

Lab Director & In-Charge

Dr. Suman Banerjee

Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science and Engineering
Indian Institute of Technology Jammu, India

Dr. Suman Banerjee received his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur and subsequently worked as a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar. His research interests span social network analysis, data management and mining, graph theory and algorithms, and parameterized complexity, with recent work focusing on fairness issues in automated decision-making systems.

Dr. Banerjee has authored over 60 research articles in premier international journals and conferences, including DEXA, ADMA, WISE, WALCOM, Theoretical Computer Science, and Theory of Computing Systems. He frequently serves as a reviewer and program committee member for prominent international conferences and journals and leads sponsored research projects on efficient algorithms for advertising slot allocation and network optimization.

Doctorate (Ph.D.) IIT Kharagpur (CSE)
Postdoctoral Fellowship IIT Gandhinagar (CSE)

Sponsored Research Initiatives

National Security Project

ISEA Project: Secure Cloud & Mobile Workflow Scheduling

As part of the Government of India's Information Security Education and Awareness (ISEA) initiative, GAN Lab designs resource-aware schedulers that drastically reduce computational latency and operational overhead while strictly safeguarding data confidentiality in distributed cloud and edge environments.

Principal Focus: Cloud Security & Scheduling Status: Active
Sponsored Algorithmic Project

Regret Minimization & Fairness in Advertisement Slot Allocation

Developing mathematically provable, submodular optimization algorithms to balance advertiser utility, user attention metrics, and fairness in offline billboard placement and dynamic social advertising platforms.
